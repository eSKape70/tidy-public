Tidy Assistant

Just ask. Tidy does the rest.

Tidy is a calm assistant for your photo library and calendar. Speak or type, and Tidy finds what you mean.

Requirements

  • An iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence, with iOS or iPadOS 27 or later.
  • Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri.
  • Siri's language set to the same language as your device, in Settings > General > Language & Region.

Common questions

Tidy says Apple Intelligence isn't available.
Check that Apple Intelligence is on and that Siri's language matches your device's language. After turning it on or changing language, it can take a few minutes for the model to download.

A photo search misses some photos.
Tidy labels your photos on your device the first time you search, which can take a while for a large library. Results fill in as the scan progresses.

Tidy can't see my photos or calendar.
Allow access for Tidy in the Settings app, under Privacy & Security > Photos, Calendars, or Reminders.
