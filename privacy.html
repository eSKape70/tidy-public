Tidy Assistant

Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

Tidy Assistant ("Tidy") is an assistant for your photo library and calendar. It is built to keep your data on your device. This policy explains what Tidy accesses, what it does with it, and the few times it uses the internet.

The short version

  • Tidy does not collect any data. The developer never receives your photos, calendar, voice, or questions.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party code.
  • Tidy changes your library or calendar only when you ask, and every deletion needs your confirmation.

What Tidy accesses on your device

Each permission is requested only when you first use the feature that needs it, and you can change it at any time in the Settings app.

  • Photos. To find, count, and organize your photos, make albums, and find similar photos. Tidy uses Apple's Vision framework on your device to label what your photos show. These labels are saved only in Tidy's private storage on your device and are deleted when you delete Tidy.
  • Calendars and Reminders. To answer questions about your schedule, add or change events and reminders you ask for, and tidy up your calendar. Events and reminders are read and changed only on your device, through Apple's calendar framework.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition. Only while you hold the microphone button, to turn your voice into a message. The recording is a temporary file that is deleted as soon as it has been transcribed.

How your requests are understood

Tidy uses Apple Intelligence, the language model built into your device, to understand your requests. Your questions are answered on your device and are not sent to the developer.

When Tidy uses the internet

  • Places. When you ask for photos from a place, such as "photos from Barcelona", only that place name is sent to Apple Maps to find where it is. Your photos' locations are compared with it on your device and never leave it.
  • iCloud Photos. Previews of photos stored only in iCloud are downloaded from your iCloud Photos, as in the Photos app.
  • Speech. When your device can't recognize your language on the device, Apple's speech recognition service transcribes the recording instead.

These services are provided by Apple and are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer receives none of this information.

Settings

Tidy saves its own settings on your device. They are deleted when you delete Tidy.

Children

Tidy does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Tidy's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
